Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does Vedryxa do?

Vedryxa is a connected growth systems company based in Hyderabad, India. We help businesses in India, the United States, the United Kingdom and Australia connect their digital marketing, website, AI automation and sales process, so more of the leads they generate turn into revenue.

How is Vedryxa different from a digital marketing agency?

A typical agency runs one piece, such as ads or SEO. We look at the full path from first click to closed deal and fix the gaps between marketing, technology and sales. Digital marketing is one of the things we offer, but the goal is a system where every part makes the others work better.

What is AI search visibility?

AI search visibility, also called answer engine optimization (AEO) or generative engine optimization (GEO), is the work of making your business easy for tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to understand, trust and recommend when buyers ask them questions.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We work with clients in India, the United States, the United Kingdom and Australia. Work is delivered remotely, with meeting times arranged to suit your time zone.

Do we need to use every service?

No. Many businesses start with one area, such as lead generation or a new website, and connect the rest over time. We recommend the order that will make the biggest difference first.

Request a strategy session through the contact page or message us on WhatsApp. We will talk through your business, your current setup and your goals, then suggest where to begin.
