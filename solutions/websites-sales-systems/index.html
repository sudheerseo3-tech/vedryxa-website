Questions

Websites and sales systems FAQ

What kind of websites does Vedryxa build?

We build business websites, landing pages and e-commerce stores that are fast, mobile-friendly, search-ready and connected to your lead capture and CRM.

Can you work with our existing CRM?

Yes. We can set up a new CRM or improve the one you already use, connect it to your website, ads and WhatsApp, and build the follow-up process around it.

What is a sales system?

A sales system is the combination of tools and steps that moves a lead from first enquiry to a closed deal: capture, qualification, follow-up, appointments and tracking. It makes results predictable instead of dependent on memory.
