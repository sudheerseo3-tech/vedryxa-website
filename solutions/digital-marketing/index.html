Questions

Digital marketing FAQ

What digital marketing services does Vedryxa offer?

Vedryxa offers SEO, AI search visibility (AEO and GEO), Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn and social media marketing, content, and conversion optimization. Every channel is connected to your website and sales follow-up, so results can be tracked from click to customer.

What is the difference between SEO and AI search visibility?

SEO helps your pages rank in traditional search results. AI search visibility helps tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews understand and recommend your business in their answers. Both matter now, and much of the groundwork overlaps.

Which is better for my business, Google Ads or Meta Ads?

It depends on how your buyers behave. Google Ads captures people already searching for what you offer, while Meta Ads creates demand among people who match your ideal customer. Many businesses use both, and we recommend a split based on your goals and budget.

How do you report results?

We report on the numbers that matter to the business, such as qualified leads, cost per lead, booked calls and revenue where it can be tracked, not just clicks and impressions.
