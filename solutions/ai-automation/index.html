AI and automation FAQ

What kind of AI automation can a small or mid-sized business use?

The most useful starting points are usually lead response, follow-up reminders, routine reporting and customer questions. These are repetitive, time-sensitive tasks where automation saves hours each week and stops leads from slipping through.

Will an AI chatbot replace my team?

No. A well-built assistant handles common questions and collects details at any hour, then hands qualified conversations to your team. Your people spend more time on the conversations that need them.

Which tools do you work with?

We choose tools to fit your business rather than forcing one platform. That can include CRMs, WhatsApp Business, Google Workspace, automation platforms and large language models from providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic and Google.

Is our data safe?

We design automations to use only the data they need, use your own accounts wherever possible, and document what each automation does so you stay in control.
